Frequently asked questions

How does receipt scanning work?
Open the Kitchen Inventory tab and tap Add Receipt, then take a photo of your grocery receipt. The items on it are read automatically and added to your inventory — no manual typing needed.
How do AI-generated recipes work?
Go to the AI Chef tab and tap "Use My Inventory" to get recipe ideas built from ingredients you already have, or type a custom request for something specific. Recipes respect the diets and allergies you set in Settings.
Can I import recipes from websites?
Yes. Paste any recipe URL into the app and it will be clipped into your recipe collection with ingredients and instructions organized for you.
How do I set dietary restrictions or allergies?
Open Settings and choose your diets (vegan, keto, paleo, gluten-free, and more) and any allergies. Every AI recipe and suggestion will respect them automatically.
How do grocery lists know what I already own?
Your grocery list checks against your kitchen inventory. If a planned item is already in your kitchen, the app flags it so you don't buy duplicates.
Can I sync my meal plan to my calendar?
Yes. In Settings, you can subscribe to your meal plan feed in Google Calendar or Apple Calendar so your planned meals show up alongside your day.
How do I track my grocery budget?
Set a monthly budget in the Grocery Budget section. Your scanned receipts count toward it automatically, with spending broken down by category and compared against previous months.
How do I delete my account or data?
You can delete your account and associated data yourself at any time in the Account section of the app, or from your account settings on airecipemanager.com.
